# Echoes of Australian Music

A data visualisation project created for FIT3179 Data Visualisation.

This project explores how Australia’s musical past survives through preserved artefacts, live music venues and audience participation.

## Research Question

**How does Australia’s musical past survive, and how does that culture continue today?**

## Visualisation Story

The webpage follows a narrative from:

**Past → Places → People**

It explores:

- preserved music memorabilia
- frequently represented creators
- themes found in historical music collections
- live music venues
- geographic patterns in music participation
- audience demographics
- frequency of live music attendance

## Data Sources

The project uses public datasets from:

- State Library of Queensland – Music Queensland collection
- City of Melbourne – Live Music Venues
- Australian Bureau of Statistics – Cultural and Creative Activities 2021–22
