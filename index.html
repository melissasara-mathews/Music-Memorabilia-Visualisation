05

What kinds of subjects dominate the preserved collection?

Subject mentions can exceed the number of music records
because one record may be assigned multiple subjects.

06

Which musical themes appear across the dated periods?

War music stands out in the 1910s collection.
Piano and dance music appears across several periods.

Interpret periods with fewer than five records cautiously.
