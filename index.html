How does live-music attendance vary across Australia?

Attendance rates vary noticeably across Australia.

Northern Territory records the highest participation.

11

How does live-music attendance differ by age and sex?

Female attendance rates are higher than male rates across all
reported age groups, although some differences are small.
Participation peaks among those aged 18–24.

12

When is live-music participation highest?

Attendance is highest among people aged 18–24, at 25.2%,
before generally declining across older age groups.
Age bands are displayed as survey categories and
are not equal in width.
